SkateDash 3D

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 24, 2026

Varala Studios ("we", "us", or "our") developed and publishes SkateDash 3D, a mobile game.

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how SkateDash 3D handles information when you use the game.

1. Information We Collect

SkateDash 3D does not collect, store, or transmit personal information.

The game does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal information.

2. Data Collection and Sharing

SkateDash 3D currently:

  • Does not collect personal information.
  • Does not collect or store user account information.
  • Does not use Firebase.
  • Does not use Google Analytics or other analytics services.
  • Does not use Crashlytics.
  • Does not use advertising networks.
  • Does not display advertisements.
  • Does not sell or share personal information with third parties.
  • Does not use location data.
  • Does not access contacts, messages, photos, or other personal files.
  • Does not require users to sign in.

3. Internet Access

The game does not require users to provide personal information over the internet.

If the game or its services are changed in the future to introduce online functionality, analytics, advertising, accounts, or other data collection, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

4. Children's Privacy

SkateDash 3D does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Because the game does not currently collect personal information, there is no personal information collected through the game for us to retain or share.

5. Third-Party Services

SkateDash 3D currently does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, social-networking, authentication, or data-collection services.

Google Play may independently process information in accordance with Google's own policies when you download or use the game through Google Play.

6. AI-Assisted Development

SkateDash 3D was developed with the assistance of artificial intelligence (AI) tools, including Cursor and ChatGPT.

These tools were used as development assistants for programming assistance, debugging, game-development workflows, design ideation, and related development activities.

The use of AI-assisted development does not mean that SkateDash 3D collects or sends player personal information to these tools. The game itself does not currently transmit player personal information to Cursor, ChatGPT, or other AI services.

7. Data Security

Because SkateDash 3D does not currently collect or store personal information, there is no player personal-information database maintained by Varala Studios.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the functionality of SkateDash 3D changes or if our privacy practices change. Any updated version will include a revised effective date.
